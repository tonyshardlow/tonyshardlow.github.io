MA52027 Year-long project

Semester

AY


Credits

20 credits


Synopsis

You will complete an individual research project over two semesters, which may be to synthesise a collection of material from any area of mathematics, to apply results and methods to new examples, to carry out computations or simulations, or to perform data analysis.


Content

Individual project supervisors provide academic direction and tutorial support. During the initial stages, a short description is written to identify the scope and goals of the project, including any necessary skills training.


Assessment

100% coursework


Prerequisites

No prerequisites
