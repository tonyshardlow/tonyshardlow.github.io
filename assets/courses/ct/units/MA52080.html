MA52080 Calculus of Variations

Semester

S2


Credits

5 credits


Synopsis

The calculus of variations is a theory on how to maximise and minimise certain quantities, with many connections to differential equations and to fundamental natural laws. You will develop your understanding of the theory and explore a range of techniques to analyse and solve the problems arising in this context.


Content

Weak derivatives, Sobolev spaces. Linear and non-linear functionals. Minima, maxima, and critical points. First variation, Euler-Lagrange equation. Statement of the Banach-Alaoglu theorem. Existence of solutions via the direct method. Applications and examples.


Assessment

Exam only
