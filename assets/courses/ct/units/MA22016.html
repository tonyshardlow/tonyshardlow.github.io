MA22016 Differential Equations & Vector Calculus

Semester

S1


Credits

10 credits


Synopsis

You will develop your understanding of two areas that are key to applied maths: ordinary differential equations and vector calculus. You will study techniques for solving and analysing systems of ordinary differential equations. You will explore the theory of vector calculus. You will see the application of the methods and theory to real-world problems. This unit will provide you with a foundation for further study in a wide range of topics in applied maths.


Content

Vector Calculus: directional derivatives; gradients; potentials; line integrals; divergence; curl; surface and volume integrals; curvilinear coordinates; integral theorems (divergence, Green’s, Stokes’). ODEs: systems of equations; solution of homogeneous linear systems; eigenvectors and generalized eigenvectors; fundamental matrices and matrix exponentials; linear and nonlinear equations in the plane; linearisation at equilibria; stability; phase planes; simple bifurcation scenarios.
