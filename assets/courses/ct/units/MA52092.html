MA52092 Stochastic Analysis

Semester

S1


Credits

10 credits


Synopsis

In this unit you will explore techniques in probability allowing you to define and analyse more advanced stochastic processes in continuous time. You will learn about Brownian motion and its path properties. Moreover, you will develop rigorously the theory of stochastic integration and Itô calculus. You will also learn about Stochastic Differential equations, including their solution theory and examples.


Content

Stochastic Processes in continuous time: Filtrations, stopping times. Brownian motion: Definition, path properties, Lévy construction, quadratic variation, the strong Markov property, the reflection principle, recurrence and transience for Brownian motion. Stochastic integration: Construction including in particular Itô isometry, Itô’s formula, Lévy characterisation of Brownian motion, Girsanov theorem. Stochastic differential equations (SDEs): existence and uniqueness of strong solutions (comparison with theory of ODEs), Markov property, generator and Fokker Planck equation, Examples: geometric Brownian motion, Ornstein Uhlenbeck process. Optional: reversible gradient diffusions and invariant measures.
