Synopsis

Algebraic Topology is the art of turning difficult problems in topology into perhaps easier problems in algebra. Beyond its intrinsic beauty, it has applications throughout mathematics from the fundamental theorem of algebra to fixed point theorems for partial differential equations. In this course, you will associate a group to a topological space; learn how to compute it and see some amazing applications.


Content

Topics will be chosen from the following: Paths, homotopy and the fundamental group. Homotopy of maps; homotopy equivalence and deformation retracts. Computation of the fundamental group and applications: Fundamental Theorem of Algebra; Brouwer Fixed Point Theorem; Borsuk-Ulam Theorem. Covering spaces. Path-lifting and homotopy lifting properties. Deck transformations. Universal covers. Covering space actions. Higher homotopy groups.
