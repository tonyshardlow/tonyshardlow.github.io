MA22035 Mathematical Biology: Ecology and Epidemiology

Semester

S2


Credits

5 credits


Synopsis

In this unit you will explore the use of applied maths to understand biological systems. You will study how to formulate, analyse and interpret appropriate mathematical models to examine ecological and epidemiological processes and phenomena. You will develop an understanding of the underlying biological theory, as well as the mathematics. This unit will give you the chance to see the extensive application of mathematics in another scientific discipline.


Content

Modelling ecological and epidemiological dynamics with ordinary differential equations. Population dynamics of single species - basic reproductive ratio; Allee effects; harvesting; maximum sustainable yield; yield-effort curves; hysteresis. Population dynamics of interacting species - predator-prey models; functional responses; paradox of enrichment; competition models; principle of competitive exclusion. Infectious diseases - contact rates and paradigms; basic reproductive ratio; epidemic threshold theorems; final epidemic size; eradication and control. Parts of the content include using and adapting code for computational model analysis.


Assessment

Exam only
