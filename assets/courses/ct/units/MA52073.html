MA52073 Advanced Dynamical Systems

Semester

S2


Credits

5 credits


Synopsis

Building on the differential equations and dynamical systems unit, you will develop mathematical theory and techniques to analyse and understand deterministic systems that change over time. You will explore methods to identify and characterise key dynamical properties. This is a rich area of mathematical study in its own right, and is applied extensively in areas including physics, biology, chemistry and economics.


Content

Linearisation and hyperbolicity. Stability. Stable Manifold Theorem. Periodic solutions and dynamics in the plane: Poincaré index; Poincaré-Bendixson Theorem. Saddle-node, transcritical, pitchfork and Hopf bifurcations. Global bifurcations;
