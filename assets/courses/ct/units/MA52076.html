MA52076 Advanced Statistical Modelling 4B

Semester

S2


Credits

10 credits


Synopsis

You will study statistical decision theory, experimental design and methods for modelling and analysing structured data. Through case studies, the course will develop your skills in choosing an appropriate method to solve an applied problem, drawing on methods introduced in the sequence of courses Statistics


Content

Decision theory: loss functions, utility functions, classical and Bayesian approaches and the relationship between them via Wald’s Complete Class Theorem. Experimental design: principles of experimental design, classical and Bayesian approaches to optimal design, computer experiments and emulators. Statistical models with a graphical basis: hierarchical models, Gaussian process. Applications: illustrations chosen from applications in functional data analysis, spatial statistics, extreme value modelling.


Assessment

50% coursework; 50% exam


Prerequisites

MA52075 Advanced Statistical Modelling 4A
