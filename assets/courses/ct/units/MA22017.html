MA22017 Groups and Rings

Semester

S2


Credits

10 credits


Synopsis

You will explore the basic algebraic structures of groups and rings, met briefly in the earlier algebra courses. The unit combines the development of structure using axioms with applications (for example, groups to geometry, and rings to polynomials), leading to some of the jewels of basic algebra, such as the study of Platonic solids and unique factorisation in polynomial rings.


Content

Elementary axiomatic theory of groups and rings. Subgroups, normal subgroups and quotient groups. Group homomorphisms and isomorphisms. Order of an element and a group. Cyclic groups. Actions, orbits and stabilisers. Cayley's theorem. Orbit partition theorem. Orbit stabiliser theorem. Integral domains, fields, characteristic. Subrings and products of rings. Homomorphisms, ideals and quotient rings. Modules. Isomorphism theorems. Fields of fractions. Polynomial rings. Maximal ideals and prime ideals. Factorisation in integral domains. Unique factorisation in principal ideal domains. Eisenstein criterion and other criteria for factorisation in polynomial rings. Modules over principal ideal domains. Further topics to me chosen from: Tensor products. Quaternions. Normed algebras.
