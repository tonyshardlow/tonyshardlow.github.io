Semester

S1


Credits

5 credits


Synopsis

In this unit you will build upon the theory introduced in Fluid Dynamics. You will develop and explore mathematical theory and techniques in order to study the behaviour of inviscid and viscous fluids in a range of different scenarios. This unit will provide you with deeper insight into the theory of fluid dynamics and continuum mechanics, with applications to engineering and the physical sciences.


Content

Navier-Stokes equations. Unidirectional flows: Couette and Poiseuille flow; streamflow. High Reynolds flow: boundary layers, viscous flow. Low Reynolds flow: slow flow past a cylinder, sphere, aerofoil. Lubrication theory: slider bearings, Hele-Shaw cells, thin film free surface flows. Additional topics may include: numerical computation, stability and Orr-Somerfield equations, porous media, asymptotic analysis.
