Semester

S1


Credits

5 credits


Synopsis

The idea behind Fourier analysis is to decompose functions as a sum of oscillatory functions, with this idea then used across the spectrum of mathematics, from signal processing (at the applied end) to number theory (at the pure end). This unit will introduce you to the rigorous theory of Fourier analysis.


Content

Topics will be chosen from the following: Fourier series. Convergence in L2, pointwise and uniform. Properties of corresponding functions. Convolution operators. Weyl’s equidistribution theorem. Fourier transform. Schwartz space. Fourier inversion and Plancherel formulae. Poisson summation formula, Heisenberg principle. Symmetry, Bessel functions. Fourier analysis on Z/nZ (finite Fourier analysis). Application to analytic number theory, Dirichlet theorem. Fourier analysis on locally compact Abelian groups, Pontryagin duality. Applications to differential equations.
