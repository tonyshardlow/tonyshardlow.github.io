MA52075 Advanced Statistical Modelling 4A

Semester

S1


Credits

10 credits


Synopsis

You will study principles of statistical inference and general asymptotic theory of statistical tests and estimates. The course will introduce advanced topics such as: re-sampling methods for inference, nonparametric methods, generalised additive models, Bayesian analysis of large, complex models. You will learn about the computational techniques used to implement some of these methods.


Content

Re-sampling methods: cross-validation, the bootstrap. Multiple testing: family-wise error rate, false discovery rate, implementation of large-scale testing. Generalised additive models: basis expansion of a function, smoothing penalties, smoothing splines, model fitting in R. Basic theory of Markov chain Monte Carlo: how to code up simple MCMC samplers in R, extensions to more advanced samplers such as ensemble-based methods, sequential Monte Carlo, and particle filters.


Assessment

50% coursework; 50% exam
