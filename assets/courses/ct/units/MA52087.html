Synopsis

This unit continues (from Methods for Differential Equations 1) the study of how to solve ordinary and partial differential equations. In this unit you will encounter equations where one often cannot write down explicitly the solution, but this unit will introduce you to techniques that nevertheless can be used to obtain useful information about properties of the solutions.


Content

Laplace/Poisson's equation: mean-value theorem and maximum principle, uniqueness, Green's integral representation, Green's functions, ubiquity in applications (e.g., via translational and rotational invariance), energy minimisation. Heat equation: maximum principle, uniqueness, solution via orthogonal series expansions (e.g., Fourier series), solution via Fourier transform (Poisson's formula for solution of Cauchy problem), Green's function and physical interpretation, viewing the heat equation as a diffusion equation.
