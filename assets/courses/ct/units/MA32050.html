MA32050 Final-year BSc Project (MA)

Semester

S2


Credits

10 credits


Synopsis

You will undertake an individual research project. You will read about your chosen topic, discuss with your academic supervisor, write a report, and give an oral presentation.


Content

The programme of study for each project will be that defined by the project description. Individual project supervisors provide academic direction and tutorial support during the project. This will include direction to appropriate academic material and skills training that may be required by the student.


Assessment

100% coursework


Prerequisites

No prerequisites


Banned combinations  

MA32028 with MA32050
