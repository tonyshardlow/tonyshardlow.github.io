MA32046 Control Theory

Semester

S1


Credits

5 credits


Synopsis

This unit will explore some of the mathematics which is relevant in an engineering context. You will learn how to model engineering systems, how to mathematically analyse those models, and how to design controllers based on those models. In the course of this, you will both apply mathematics that you learned earlier in your programme and learn new mathematical techniques.


Content

Input-state-output system. Transfer function, frequency response, step response, impulse response. Modelling of systems in different physical domains (including mechanical and electrical). Controller design.

Exam only
