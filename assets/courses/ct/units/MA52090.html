MA52090 Numerical Solution of Evolutionary Equations (in Y3 or Y4)

Semester

S1


Credits

5 credits


Synopsis

You will explore convergence theory and linear stability theory for the numerical approximation of ODEs and evolutionary PDEs.


Content

Topics chosen from: Solution of initial-value problems for ODEs by linear multistep methods, Runge-Kutta methods, splitting methods and exponential integrators; techniques for finding the convergence and stability properties of numerical methods; numerical methods for parabolic and hyperbolic PDEs.


Assessment

Exam only


Prerequisites

MA22037 Numerical Analysis
MA22021 Partial Differential Equations
