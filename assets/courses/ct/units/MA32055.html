MA32055 Groups and their Representations

Semester

S2


Credits

10 credits


Synopsis

You will explore one of the main branches of Algebra, Group Theory, that can be seen as the mathematical theory that deals with symmetry.


Content

Semidirect products, finite nilpotent groups and their characterisations, the Frattini subgroup and the Burnside Basis Theorem, solvable groups, Some Sylow Theory and material on simple groups. Group algebras, their modules and associated representations. Maschke's theorem and complete reducibility. Irreducible representations and Schur's lemma. Decomposition of the regular representation. Character theory and orthogonality theorems. Further topics may include: Burnside's (p,q)-Theorem, Burnside's normal p-complement theorem, Finite p-groups, character theory for abelian groups, Iwasawa's lemma and the simplicity e.g. of PSL2(Fp), Induced representations and Frobenius reciprocity.
