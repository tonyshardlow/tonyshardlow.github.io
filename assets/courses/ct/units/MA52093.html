MA52093 Theory of PDEs

Semester

S1


Credits

5 credits


Synopsis

In this unit you will study the properties of solutions to partial differential equations (PDEs). You will explore a range of methods and techniques that are indispensable for their rigorous analysis.


Content

Basic theory of Sobolev spaces, Poincaré inequality. Existence of weak solutions of elliptic PDEs via Lax–Milgram. Maximum principles for elliptic equations with bounded coefficients, Hopf boundary point lemma. Further topics, which might include the method of moving planes, linear and semilinear parabolic equations.


Assessment

Exam only
