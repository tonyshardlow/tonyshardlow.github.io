Synopsis

This unit will combine sophisticated analytic ideas with the power of linear algebra, giving rise to a theory that is more than the sum of its parts.


Content

Revision of Banach spaces and bounded linear operators. Examples. Uniform Boundedness Theorem. Open Mapping Theorem. Closed Graph Theorem. Zorn's Lemma. Hahn-Banach Theorem. Canonical embedding, reflexivity. Banach-Alaoglu Theorem. Applications and further topics.

Exam only
