MA32028 Applied Data Science

Semester

S2


Credits

5 credits


Synopsis

You will complete a project with a large data set, including the planning and design of studies, through practical analysis to the reporting of results.


Content

Data exploration; Data mining and data analytics; Data transformations; Analysis of large, structured complex datasets; Model selection, verification of assumptions and assessment of performance in a real-world application; Presentation of results in an appropriate format.


Assessment

100% coursework


Prerequisites

MA22019 Introduction to Data Science
MA22015 Statistics 2B
MA22018 Machine Learning 1

Alternative prerequisites

MA20227 Statistics 2B


MA20277 Introduction to Data Science


MA20278 Machine Learning 1


Banned combinations
