MA52086 Inverse Problems and Numerical Optimisation

Semester

S2


Credits

10 credits


Synopsis

Inverse problems arise from the need to obtain information about an unknown object of interest from indirect measurements. Typical applications include medical imaging (e.g., computed tomography, magnetic resoinance imaging), non-destructive testing, and geosciences, to name but a few. The common feature for many inverse problems is their sensitivity to small errors in the observed data, which renders naive numerical inversion methods unstable and necessitates special regularisation methods that can stabilise the inversion. In this course we will study such regularisation techniques as well as numerical optimisation methods that can be used to solve the resulting variational problems.


Content

Inverse problems, ill-posedness. Regularisation (e.g., iterative regularisation, variational regularisation). Basic concepts of convex analysis. Convex non-smooth optimisation methods.
