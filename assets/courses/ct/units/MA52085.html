MA52085 Galois Theory

Semester

S1


Credits

5 credits


Synopsis

Galois theory originates from the classical problem of solving polynomial equations using algebraic methods. In this course you will learn about a beautiful close interplay between Group Theory and Field Theory culminating in the Fundamental Theorem of Galois Theory with a number of applications including Galois' Theorem about when a polynomial is solvable by radicals.


Content

Field extensions. Algebraic closure. Splitting fields. Normal and separable field extensions. Galois groups. The Galois correspondence and the fundamental theorem of Galois Theory. Solvable groups and the theorem of Galois on solvability of polynomials. The fundamental theorem of algebra. Finite fields.
