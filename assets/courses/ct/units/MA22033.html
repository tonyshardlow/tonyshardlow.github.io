MA22033 Analysis 2B

Semester

S2


Credits

5 credits


Synopsis

Continuing from Analysis 2A, you will extend the key ideas and methods from mathematical analysis to study functions of several variables.


Content

Topology of Euclidean spaces: Bolzano-Weierstrass, Heine-Borel, and Weierstrass theorems. Decreasing sets. Differentiation of functions of several variables: Frechet derivative, partial derivatives, Jacobian matrix and directional derivatives. Chain rule. Symmetry of the second derivative. Higher order partial derivatives and the Hessian matrix. Stationary points and second derivative test. Taylor’s theorem. Inverse and implicit function theorems for continuously differentiable maps, surfaces (submanifolds) defined by equations.


Assessment

Exam only
