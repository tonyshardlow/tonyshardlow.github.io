MA52072 Advanced Applied Maths

Semester

S2


Credits

5 credits


Synopsis

In this unit you will build on the mathematical theory and techniques you learnt in other applied maths units. You will investigate more advanced methods, and use them to study classical and topical problems in scientific areas such as physics, engineering and biology. This unit will provide you with a deeper insight into the applications of mathematics in science and engineering.


Content

Methods and applications of partial differential equations; mathematical and scientific skills for tackling advanced applied mathematical challenges in academic and industrial settings. Wave equations; diffusion equations. Separation of variables; method of characteristics; weak solutions; computational methods. Mathematical and scientific communication. Application areas may include: mechanical engineering, environmental science, biological science, materials science.


Assessment

100% coursework


Prerequisites

MA22021 Partial Differential Equations
