MA32024 Statistical Modelling and Data Analytics 3B

Semester

S2


Credits

10 credits


Synopsis

You will learn about models for data with complex structures such as time series spatial data, and linear models with random effects. You will develop skills in data analysis through case studies involving these types of data.


Content

Probability models for time series. Estimating the autocorrelation function and fitting ARIMA models. Forecasting: Exponential smoothing, forecasting from ARIMA models. Conditional independence properties of the multivariate normal distribution. Graphical representations of multivariate data. Multivariate models with special structure such as Gaussian random fields and spatial models. Gaussian random fields; Markov random fields. Analysis of spatial data and other structured data sets. Application of the above methods to a variety of real-life statistical problems


Assessment

50% coursework; 50% exam
